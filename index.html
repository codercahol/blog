Toward cheaper, more programmable manufacturing

I work on solving the bottlenecks between science and scalable manufacturing. Past work on bioprocesses, instrumentation, high-throughput characterization, and programmable materials.

Coherent Matter Moonshot

I’m developing a manufacturing platform for materials with designed structures spanning nanometers to meters, starting with optical films. This effort is part of Renaissance Philanthropy’s BiTS fellowship with SPRIND.

Accelerating materials testing

I’m building a startup to accelerate materials testing. Currently in stealth.

Background

I have a Biophysics PhD from Stanford and a Molecular Engineering BSc from UChicago.

My work has spanned microbial metabolism and physiology, reactor instrumentation and automation, transport phenomena in process scale-up, computational modeling, programmable materials, and hands-on fabrication.

I am especially interested in unlocking materials innovation: how can advanced materials transition from idea to scale more than 10× faster?

I play by tinkering and spending time in the outdoors.
