Toward cheaper, more programmable manufacturing

Coherent Matter

Programming multi-scale material structure, from nanometers to meters, is possible in controlled lab environments, but infeasible anywhere else.

Coherent Matter is developing a platform to unlock the inexpensive manufacturing of multi-scale materials, by directing the assembly of ordered liquids on surfaces with industrially-scalable techniques. Programmable material structure can open doors to amazing new materials for spectropolarimetry, hierarchically-structured electrodes, smart separations membranes, robotic skins, artificial tendons and more.

Development of the program was supported by Renaissance Philanthropy's Big if True Science (BiTS) Accelerator in partnership with SPRIND. The concept note is available below. A pitch recording will be available by December 2026.

Automated Materials Validation

Next-generation tech in outer space, undergound, and in the ocean relies on new materials. However, these materials are stuck as lab demos because physical validation currently takes years. I am building an automated platform to rapidly validate new materials, in order to cut time to deployment from years to weeks.
