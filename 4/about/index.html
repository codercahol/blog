About

I am a builder, scientist, and technical generalist working to solve the bottlenecks between science and scalable manufacturing.

I have a Biophysics PhD from Stanford and a Molecular Engineering BSc from UChicago.

My work has spanned microbial metabolism and physiology, reactor instrumentation and automation, transport phenomena in process scale-up, computational modeling, programmable materials, and hands-on fabrication.

I am especially interested in unlocking materials innovation: how can advanced materials transition from idea to scale more than 10× faster?

I play by tinkering and spending time in the outdoors.
