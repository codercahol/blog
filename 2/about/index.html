About

I am a builder, scientist, and technical generalist working on the bottlenecks between laboratory science and scalable manufacturing.

My work spans biology, instrumentation, process systems, modeling, materials, and hands-on fabrication. I am especially interested in the systems that make physical technologies easier to measure, validate, scale, and manufacture.

I trained in biophysics and have worked across microbial physiology, bioreactors, spectroscopy, automation, and process-oriented measurement. The common thread is translation: taking fragile lab observations and turning them into systems that can survive contact with real constraints.

The tinkering side matters. Repairs, fixtures, rigs, and small builds are part of how I think. They keep the broader manufacturing ambition grounded in real materials, failure modes, and practical tradeoffs.
